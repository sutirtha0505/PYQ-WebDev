About Diwali

Diwali, also known as the Festival of Lights, is one of the most popular festivals celebrated in India. People decorate their homes with lamps, candles and colourful decorations.

Families celebrate Diwali by exchanging sweets and gifts, wearing new clothes and enjoying beautiful fireworks. The festival symbolizes the victory of light over darkness and good over evil.
